Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Applies to the Taleberry app for iOS and Android

Taleberry makes short bedtime tales in which your child is the hero. The app is used by parents. Children do not create accounts. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. Questions: dummycash17@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Your account: the email address and, if you share it, the name that Apple or Google gives us when you sign in (Apple may give a private relay address)To create your account and keep your tales together
Child profiles you create: first name, age, interests, and the look you choose for the hero (hair, skin tone, outfit and similar options). No photos.To write tales about your child
Tales: the story ideas you type, and the generated text, pictures and narrationTo show and replay your tales
Subscription status (from the App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat)To give you the tales included in your plan
A notification token for your device, if you allow notificationsTo tell you when a tale is ready

We do not collect your location, contacts or photos, we do not use advertising identifiers, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

2. AI services that make each tale

Before the first tale, the app asks for your consent and shows this list. When you create a tale, we send these companies only what they need:

  • OpenAI writes the story and paints the pictures. It receives your child's age and interests, whether the hero is a girl or a boy (if you chose it), the story idea you typed, and a description of the hero's look. It does not receive your child's name: the story is written with a placeholder and the name is added on our servers afterwards.
  • Tales are read aloud by your phone's own voice. The text stays on your phone and is not sent to any voice service.

We never send a surname, a photo, your email or your location to these services. They act as our service providers and may not use this data to train their models. Where a provider offers zero data retention, we use it. If we change providers, the app asks for your consent again.

3. Children's privacy

Taleberry is for parents. We collect information about a child only from their parent, after the parent's consent, and only to make that child's tales. You can see, change or delete a child's profile and tales at any time in the app. A child cannot buy anything, change settings or contact anyone through the app.

4. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Your account, profiles and tales are stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider. Data is encrypted in transit. Each parent can reach only their own data. Pictures and narration are kept in private storage and opened through short-lived links. Your sign-in session is kept in your device's secure keychain.

We do not sell personal data and we do not show ads.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete a tale, a child or your whole account in the app (Profile, Delete account), or without the app as described on Delete your account. Account deletion removes your account, children, tales, pictures and narration from our systems; copies in backups are removed within 30 days. Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to dummycash17@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or COPPA), you may have further rights, including withdrawing consent; withdrawing consent stops new tales from being made.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
