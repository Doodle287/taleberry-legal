Delete your Taleberry account

Taleberry · bedtime tales where your child is the hero · developer: Taleberry

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Taleberry and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the list of what will be removed, then press and hold to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Send an email to dummycash17@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with (or tell us that address), with the subject Delete my Taleberry account. If you signed in with Apple's "Hide My Email", write from that Apple relay address or tell us which one it is. We confirm and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and name from Apple or Google)
  • All child profiles and their hero pictures
  • All tales, their text, pictures and narration
  • Notification tokens of your devices
  • Your subscription record at our payment provider (RevenueCat)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in encrypted backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Purchase records that Apple, Google or tax law require us to keep may be retained by those stores, not by us.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

You can also delete a single tale or a single child in the app without deleting your account.
